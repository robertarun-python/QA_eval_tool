The practice app will be written as code with three layers of helper functions the candidate's test can call:
- UI: what a user does and sees (open a page, log in, fill in and submit, read what's shown: the current page, the visible message, lists and details on screen).
- API: the same operations called directly, returning structured results (e.g. {"ok": false, "error": "..."}).
- Database: direct reads of what was really stored, for verification, plus cleanup.
Plus setup() (reset to logged out, starting page, no message) and teardown(id=None) (undo anything a test created, given its id).
